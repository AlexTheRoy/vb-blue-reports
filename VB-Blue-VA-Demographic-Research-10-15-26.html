Demographic profile of all 886 populated Virginia ZIP codes: household income, homeownership, home values, age and vehicles, built from the U.S. Census Bureau's ACS 2020–2024 five-year estimates (released December 2025), the most current ZIP-level data published. Hampton Roads, your largest lead area, is called out throughout: the seven cities of Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Portsmouth, Suffolk, Hampton and Newport News. Northern Virginia (Fairfax, Prince William and Loudoun counties) and the Richmond area (Richmond City, Henrico and Chesterfield) also supply many of your leads and are called out alongside. Section 07 lines the state up against the 1,060 GOAL leads delivered from July 22 to October 15, 2026 (474 Home and 586 Auto). The companion spreadsheet carries every ZIP and every column; the hurricane and flood report covers the risk geography.

Data & files

  • SourceU.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2020–2024 five-year estimates (released December 2025), tables B01002, B01003, B08201, B11001, B11007, B19013, B25001, B25002, B25003, B25004, B25077. Geography: 2020 ZIP Code Tabulation Areas.
  • Coverage886 populated Virginia ZIPs. PO-box-only ZIPs have no resident population and no Census profile; traffic from them resolves to the surrounding ZIP. Median income is published for 805 ZIPs and median home value for 790.
  • Top-codingThe Census caps medians at “$250,000 or more” for income (7 ZIPs) and “$2,000,000 or more” for home value (0 ZIPs); those ZIPs are shown at the cap.
  • GeographyVirginia has 95 counties and 38 independent cities. The cities (Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Richmond and the rest) are not part of any county, so each is treated as its own county-equivalent, as the Census Bureau does; the county column in the spreadsheet reads “Virginia Beach city” or “Henrico County”. Each ZIP is assigned to the county or city holding the largest share of its land (2020 ZCTA-to-county relationship file); 4 ZIPs whose land lies mostly in a neighboring state are left out, and 19 localities, all small cities, are not the largest part of any ZIP. The 133 localities are grouped into nine regions: Hampton Roads, Northern Virginia, the Richmond Region, Fredericksburg & Piedmont, the Eastern Shore & Bay Counties (Northern Neck and Middle Peninsula), the Shenandoah Valley & Central, Roanoke & Lynchburg, Southside and Southwest Virginia. The city column is left blank: no city file was used, so ZIPs are identified by county or independent city.
  • LeadsAll 1,060 GOAL leads for this account, Jul 22 to Oct 15, 2026 (474 Home, 586 Auto), counted by ZIP. 474 of 474 Home leads and 582 of 586 Auto leads matched a populated Virginia ZIP. Twelve weeks is a modest sample; the lead splits will steady as volume builds.
  • PrecisionAll values carry ACS margins of error and are strongest for larger ZIPs. Blank means not published, never zero.
  • FilesVB-Blue-VA-ZIP-Data-10-15-26.csv · every ZIP, every column, with the hurricane and flood tier from the companion report.
